Entering a show

Step 1 of 5: Before you enter

Before you enter

  • Read the show advert and check the entry deadline.
  • Entries are by email only. Contact the show secretary about a week before the show, and before the close of entries time on the advert.
  • You can find the show secretary's contact details on the Show Calendar.
  • All show staff are volunteers. Show secretaries may not reply until the week of the show, and they confirm entries after the entry deadline.

Check the show on the NFRS show calendar for the deadline and whether it is dual entry.

What kind of show is it?

No entry fee and all guests are welcome. You do not need to contact the show secretary much before the show unless you have questions.

There is usually an entry fee. You can sometimes get exhibitor passes by sending an SAE to the show secretary by a set date. Read the advert well before the show for any extra entry rules.

Have you claimed a stud name with the NFRS?

Only answer Yes if you have claimed your stud. If you have a rattery name but have not claimed a stud, answer No and enter under your own name. The show secretary needs this to write the cards and put your pets in the right class.

Your rats

Add each rat you are bringing. If you are not sure of the class, choose "Not sure" and describe the rat in full: colour, markings, coat and ears. Use the NFRS varieties pages to check.

Anything else

Hire a show tank?

£5 each, max 2 per exhibitor. Tanks are limited. Bring your own suitable substrate, bedding material, dry food and a water bottle.
